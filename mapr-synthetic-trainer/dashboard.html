Mapr synthetic user training

Offline. Real Landmark Hunters places, simulated travelers, the production Mapr code. connecting…

What you're watching: Mapr is learning from 10,000 made-up travelers. Each one rated 50 real places. Mapr studies 35 of those ratings, then gets quizzed on places it hasn't seen: "This traveler loved one of these two and not the other. Which one should we suggest?" ✓ means Mapr suggested the place they rated higher; ✗ means it got it backwards. Every desk shows a real question from the model training right now.
