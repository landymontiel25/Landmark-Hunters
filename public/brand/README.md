# Brand assets for outside ratings

`tripadvisor-logo.svg` goes here: Tripadvisor's official "Ollie" logo (the
primary logo on light backgrounds), downloaded from
https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/logo-guidelines. Do not redraw or recolor
it. Until this file exists, the app hides every Tripadvisor rating, because
Tripadvisor's display rules require the logo beside the rating.

## Google Maps logo

Google prefers its Google Maps logo over the text "Google Maps" beside its
ratings. Download Google's attribution assets zip
(https://developers.google.com/static/maps/documentation/images/Google_Maps_Attribution_Assets.zip)
and copy two SVGs here, unchanged:

- `google-maps-logo-on-dark.svg`: the non-outlined logo made for dark
  backgrounds (white text).
- `google-maps-logo-on-light.svg`: the non-outlined logo made for light
  backgrounds (dark gray text).

Until both files exist, the app shows the text "Google Maps" instead.
